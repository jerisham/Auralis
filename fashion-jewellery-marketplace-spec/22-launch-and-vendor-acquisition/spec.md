# 22 — Launch & Vendor Acquisition

**Phase:** P1 · **Depends on:** all P1 modules · **Status:** Draft

## 1. Purpose
A marketplace fails if it launches empty. Bring vendors and products in **first**, run a closed pilot, then open to the public with enough choice to impress.

## 2. Launch stages
| Stage | Who | Entry criteria | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpha (internal)** | Team + friends as customers; 3–5 friendly vendors | M4 done in staging | All critical journeys pass with real money (small amounts) |
| **Pilot (invite-only)** | 15–25 vendors; customers by invite/waitlist | ≥ 500 live products; legal pages live; 18 signed off | KPIs below for 4 weeks |
| **Public launch** | Everyone | Pilot KPIs met; ≥ 1,000 products; ≥ 30 active vendors | — |

Pilot KPIs: payment success ≥ 95%; vendor median accept < 6 h; dispatch on time ≥ 90%; dispute rate ≤ 3%; ≥ 4.2★ average delivery rating; 0 Sev1 incidents in last 2 weeks.

## 3. Vendor acquisition playbook
- **Target list:** 200 Instagram fashion-jewellery sellers (5k–100k followers), mix of styles (oxidised, kundan, minimal western, handmade beads) and cities (Jaipur, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Surat).
- **Offer for founding vendors:** reduced commission for first 3–6 months, "Founding Seller" badge, featured placement, free product photography day in 2 cities.
- **Onboarding support:** WhatsApp group for founding sellers; 20-min video call to set up store; bulk upload done-with-you.
- **Funnel targets:** 200 contacted → 60 interested → 30 applied → 20 live.

## 4. Customer acquisition (pilot)
- Founding vendors share their store link/QR with followers (UTM-tracked) — "Shop my store + discover 20 more sellers".
- Micro-influencer try-on reels (10–20 creators) using platform looks.
- Launch offer funded by platform (e.g., free shipping on first order) — platform-wide coupon (P2 coupon module or manual credit).
- Waitlist landing page with referral position boost.

## 5. Operational readiness
- Support: email + WhatsApp support line, response within 4 business hours; FAQ live.
- Ops: daily review of SLA breaches, claims, reconciliation mismatches.
- Finance: weekly payout review during pilot.
- Comms: launch announcement, vendor newsletter template.

## 6. Go/No-go checklist (public launch)
All module sign-offs; pen test clean; CA & lawyer sign-off; load test passed; runbooks + on-call live; pilot KPIs met; content: ≥ 1,000 products with compliant photos; homepage curated.
