# Open Questions & Decisions

Answer these before or during M0. **Blocker** = development of the listed module cannot finish without it.
Record the answer and date in the "Decision" column; update the affected `spec.md`.

| ID | Question | Recommendation | Blocks | Priority | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | Brand name, domain, logo | Short, ownable name with an available .com/.in domain and Instagram handle. Check trademark (IP India) before committing. | 01, 02 | Blocker | **Auralis** (17 Nov 2026). Domain/Instagram/trademark still to verify. |
| Q-02 | Platform fee model | % commission on item price (e.g. 15–20%), configurable per category and per vendor. Optional small fixed fee per order. Avoid listing fees at launch — lowers vendor entry barrier. | 13 | Blocker | **15% commission** on item price; no listing fee at launch (17 Nov 2026). |
| Q-03 | Payout tranches | Tranche 1 = 75% of vendor net on **Dispatched** (with valid tracking). Tranche 2 = remaining 25% after **Delivered + return window**. Matches your ₹500 → ₹100 fee → ₹300 + ₹100 example. | 13 | Blocker | **75% on dispatch / 25% after delivery + return window** (17 Nov 2026). |
| Q-04 | Who pays payment-gateway fees (~2%)? | Platform absorbs them out of its commission (simpler for vendors to understand). | 13 | Blocker | **Platform absorbs** gateway fees out of the 15% commission (17 Nov 2026). |
